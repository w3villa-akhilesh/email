DATE AND DAY CALCULATION REFERENCE GUIDE

CALENDAR STRUCTURE

DAYS OF THE WEEK (7 days per week):
1. Monday    (Day 1 of week)
2. Tuesday   (Day 2 of week)
3. Wednesday (Day 3 of week)
4. Thursday  (Day 4 of week)
5. Friday    (Day 5 of week)
6. Saturday  (Day 6 of week)
7. Sunday    (Day 7 of week)

DAY SEQUENCE (What comes after what):
- Monday → Tuesday → Wednesday → Thursday → Friday → Saturday → Sunday → Monday (repeats)
- After Sunday comes Monday (week restarts)

MONTHS OF THE YEAR (12 months):
Month 01: January    - 31 days
Month 02: February   - 28 days (29 days in leap years)
Month 03: March      - 31 days
Month 04: April      - 30 days
Month 05: May        - 31 days
Month 06: June       - 30 days
Month 07: July       - 31 days
Month 08: August     - 31 days
Month 09: September  - 30 days
Month 10: October    - 31 days
Month 11: November   - 30 days
Month 12: December   - 31 days

MONTH SEQUENCE (What comes after what):
- January → February → March → April → May → June → July → August → September → October → November → December → January (repeats)
- After December comes January (year increments by 1)

LEAP YEAR RULE:
- A year is a leap year if:
  * It is divisible by 4 AND
  * If divisible by 100, it must also be divisible by 400
- Examples: 2024 is a leap year, 2025 is NOT a leap year, 2000 was a leap year, 1900 was NOT

DATE CALCULATION RULES

STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION METHOD:
1. Always start from Today_date (YYYY-MM-DD) and Today_day
2. Count forward or backward the required number of days
3. Account for month boundaries (when days exceed month's total)
4. Account for year boundaries (when month exceeds December)
5. Calculate the resulting day of the week

RELATIVE DATE CALCULATIONS:

1. TOMORROW:
   - Add 1 day to Today_date
   - If current_day = Monday, tomorrow = Tuesday
   - If current_day = Sunday, tomorrow = Monday (next week)

3. "NEXT [WEEKDAY]":
   - Find the NEXT occurrence of that weekday AFTER today
   - Must be at least 1 day in the future (minimum: tomorrow if tomorrow is that weekday)
   - Count days forward from today until you reach that weekday
   
   Example: Today = Friday, November 7, 2025
   - "next Monday"    = Nov 10 (3 days forward: Fri→Sat→Sun→Mon)
   - "next Tuesday"   = Nov 11 (4 days forward)
   - "next Wednesday" = Nov 12 (5 days forward)
   - "next Thursday"  = Nov 13 (6 days forward)
   - "next Friday"    = Nov 14 (7 days forward, NOT today)
   - "next Saturday"  = Nov 15 (8 days forward, NOT tomorrow)
   - "next Sunday"    = Nov 16 (9 days forward)

4. "THIS [WEEKDAY]":
   - If the weekday hasn't occurred yet this week: use the upcoming occurrence
   - If the weekday already passed this week: treat as "next [weekday]"

5. "IN X DAYS" or "X DAYS FROM NOW":
   - Add X days to Today_date
   - Example: Today = Nov 7, "in 5 days" = Nov 12

7. "NEXT WEEK":
   - Add 7 days to Today_date
   - Example: Today = Nov 7, "next week" = Nov 14


EXAMPLES (Assume Today = Friday, Nov 7, 2025):

- "tomorrow": Nov 8, 2025 (Saturday)
- "next Monday": Nov 10, 2025 (Monday)
- "next Saturday": Nov 15, 2025 (Saturday, not this week)
- "next Friday": Nov 14, 2025 (Friday, never today)
- "in 10 days": Nov 17, 2025 (Monday)
- "What day is Nov 30, 2025?": Sunday



CRITICAL REMINDERS

1. ALWAYS use Today_date and Today_day as your ONLY reference point
2. "Next [weekday]" means the NEXT occurrence AFTER today (never today itself)
3. Count days carefully when crossing month boundaries
4. A week has exactly 7 days
5. After Sunday comes Monday
6. After December comes January (increment year)
7. Show your counting steps when calculating to ensure accuracy
8. Verify your final answer by counting from the start date
